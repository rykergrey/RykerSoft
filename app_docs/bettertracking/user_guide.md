## 1. Journal Overview
Switch between Day, Week, and Month journal views. Filter by domain (Food, Supplement, Exercise, Measurement, Lifestyle, Notes) to inspect habits and trends.

## 2. Quick Logging & AI
- Tap **Quick Log** and describe what you ate or snap a meal photo.
- The AI automatically extracts ingredients, portion sizes, calories, and macronutrients into your staging tray.

## 3. Ingredient & Combo Library
Create custom single ingredients or reusable combo meals to log frequent recipes with a single tap.

## 4. Calorie Engine & Targets
The adaptive calorie engine analyzes your daily weigh-ins and calculates baseline metabolic rate targets automatically.

## 5. AI Coach & Reports
Ask the AI Coach questions about your progress or trigger background health reports for actionable insights on nutrition and fitness goals.
