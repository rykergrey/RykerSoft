## 1. Overview & Layout
Synthing combines a playhead mini-map top bar, dual synth controls, performance keyboard, and a full continuous arrangement timeline.

## 2. Synth Sound Engines
- **Synth A**: Subtractive oscillator with pulse-width modulation, cutoff filter, and envelope generators.
- **Synth B**: Secondary voice with chorus, delay, and pitch modulation.

## 3. Playing & Recording
- Tap pads on the Chord Grid or keys on the Melody Keyboard to trigger notes.
- Use **LATCH** or **TOGGLE** modes for continuous drone soundscapes.
- Enable **ARPEGGIATOR** for rhythmic step sequences synced to project BPM.

## 4. Piano Roll & Editing
- Switch to the Piano Roll view to draw, resize, and quantize notes.
- Use 100% Bird's Eye view to zoom out and inspect the full song arrangement.

## 5. Project Management
Save project sketches locally on device with automatic visual arrangement preview cards on the Project Manager screen.
