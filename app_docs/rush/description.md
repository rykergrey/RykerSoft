# Rush Companion

An unofficial comprehensive Rush band companion for Android — browse full discography, read lyrics, explore liner notes, inspect tour timelines, and build your personal collection of song ratings, pins, and YouTube links.

## Key Features
- **Catalog & Discography**: High-resolution album artwork, decade filtering, and studio vs live album groupings.
- **Song & Album Explorer**: Full lyrics, liner notes, member credits, awards, and track video aggregators.
- **My Collection**: Personal song pins, custom ratings, favorite lyric quote highlights, and notes.
- **Band History & Timeline**: Interactive timeline from 1968 to present covering albums, tours, and member milestones.
