## 1. Navigating Discography
Filter Rush albums by decade (70s, 80s, 90s, 2000s+) or by release type (Studio Albums, Live Recordings, Compilations).

## 2. Album & Song Details
- Tap any album to view track lists, liner notes, recording credits, and awards.
- Tap any song title to access lyrics, appearances, and related video performances.

## 3. My Collection
- Pin your favorite albums and tracks for instant access from the home screen.
- Rate tracks on a 5-star scale and add custom personal notes or lyric quotes.

## 4. Videos & Media
Explore the Album Videos tab to watch official music videos, live performances, and fan covers linked directly to YouTube.

## 5. Band History & Timeline
Browse the interactive timeline to trace Geddy Lee, Alex Lifeson, and Neil Peart through equipment evolutions, landmark concerts, and historical album eras.
